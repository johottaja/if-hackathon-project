About If

If is the largest insurance company in the Nordics, with around 4.6 million customers, both individuals and businesses. We insure one in five homes and passenger cars in the Nordics and offer personal and property insurance for individuals and businesses. We have 10,000 employees, spread across several business areas and countries, who together ensure that our private and business customers receive the right help. 

Thanks to our size and breadth, we can be close to our customers and offer reliable insurance. Our promise to our customers is the core of our identity – If helps a lot. 

If is wholly owned by the Finnish company Sampo, which is listed on the Nasdaq stock exchange in Helsinki, Stockholm, and Copenhagen. In 2025, Topdanmark became part of If.
